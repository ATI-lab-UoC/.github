# Artificial Intelligence and Translational Imaging Lab

Welcome to the GitHub page of the **Artificial Intelligence and Translational Imaging Lab**.

Our group develops and evaluates artificial intelligence methods for medical
imaging, with a particular focus on clinical translation, multimodal AI,
radiomics, deep learning and generative AI.

## 🔬 Research Areas

- Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging
- Radiomics
- Deep Learning
- Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Multimodal AI
- Oncologic Imaging
- Musculoskeletal Imaging
- Translational Imaging
- Reproducible and Trustworthy AI

## 💻 Lab Software & Code

This page provides open-source implementations, research software and
code developed by members of our laboratory.
